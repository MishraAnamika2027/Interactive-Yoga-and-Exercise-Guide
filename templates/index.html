Trikonasana

Back Pain Shoulders Hips

Benefits

Strengthens legs and arms, improves digestion, stretches spine and hips, relieves back pain, and reduces stress and anxiety.

Steps

  1. Stand with feet 3-4 feet apart.
  2. Turn right foot out 90° and left foot slightly inward.
  3. Extend arms parallel to the floor.
  4. Bend to the right, hand to shin or floor.
  5. Extend left arm upward, gaze at left hand.
  6. Hold 30 seconds, repeat other side.

Precautions

Avoid with low blood pressure, diarrhea, or headache. Modify if you have neck issues.

Virabadrasana

Legs Hips Shoulders

Benefits

Strengthens legs and ankles, stretches hips and shoulders, improves balance and stamina, and enhances focus and determination.

Steps

  1. Stand with feet wide apart.
  2. Turn right foot out 90°.
  3. Bend right knee over ankle.
  4. Extend arms parallel to the floor.
  5. Gaze over right hand.
  6. Hold 30-60 seconds, switch sides.

Precautions

Avoid with high blood pressure or heart issues. Be cautious with knee injuries.

Recommended

Vrikshasana

Balance Legs Posture

Benefits

Improves balance and stability, strengthens legs and core, enhances focus and concentration, and improves posture.

Steps

  1. Shift weight onto left leg.
  2. Place right foot on inner thigh or calf.
  3. Bring hands to prayer at chest.
  4. Raise arms overhead once steady.
  5. Hold 30 seconds with steady breath.
  6. Switch sides and repeat.

Precautions

Avoid with recent knee or hip injury. Use wall support if needed.

Bhujangasana

Back Pain Spine Chest

Benefits

Strengthens spine and lower back, opens chest and lungs, improves blood circulation, and relieves back pain and stiffness.

Steps

  1. Lie prone with legs extended.
  2. Place palms beneath shoulders.
  3. Press into palms to lift chest.
  4. Keep elbows slightly bent.
  5. Gaze upward, hold 15-30 seconds.
  6. Lower slowly to the mat.

Precautions

Avoid if pregnant or with back injury. Do not overextend the spine.

Recommended

Sukhasana

Stress Relief Meditation Posture

Benefits

Calms the mind, reduces stress and anxiety, improves posture, strengthens back muscles, and opens hips.

Steps

  1. Sit on the floor or a cushion.
  2. Cross legs comfortably.
  3. Rest hands on knees.
  4. Lengthen spine and relax shoulders.
  5. Close eyes, breathe deeply.
  6. Hold for 5-10 minutes.

Precautions

Use cushioning if hips are tight. Avoid if you have severe knee problems.

Recommended

Chakrasana

Back Pain Spine Flexibility

Benefits

Strengthens back and spine, opens chest and lungs, stimulates thyroid and pituitary glands, and increases flexibility.

Steps

  1. Lie on back, bend knees hip-width.
  2. Place palms beside ears.
  3. Press into hands and feet.
  4. Lift hips and chest.
  5. Straighten arms if possible.
  6. Hold 5-15 seconds, lower gently.

Precautions

Advanced pose — avoid with back, wrist, or blood pressure issues. Warm up thoroughly.

Balasana

Back Pain Stress Relief Relaxation

Benefits

Gently stretches back and hips, relieves back and neck pain, calms the mind, and reduces stress and fatigue.

Steps

  1. Kneel and sit back on heels.
  2. Separate knees hip-width apart.
  3. Fold forward, forehead to floor.
  4. Extend arms forward or alongside the body.
  5. Breathe deeply and relax.
  6. Hold 1-5 minutes.

Precautions

Avoid when pregnant or with knee injury. Support forehead for comfort.

Recommended

Shavasana

Stress Relief Relaxation Recovery

Benefits

Deeply relaxes body and mind, reduces stress and anxiety, improves memory and concentration, and lowers blood pressure.

Steps

  1. Lie flat on your back.
  2. Allow legs to fall open naturally.
  3. Place arms beside body, palms up.
  4. Close eyes and relax each muscle.
  5. Breathe naturally.
  6. Hold 5-15 minutes.

Precautions

Use a blanket for comfort if cool. Stay present to avoid drifting to sleep.
